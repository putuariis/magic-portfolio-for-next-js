## Overview

Led through MyCarbonSteps × Carbon Academy, the Carbon Vlogger Workshop & Challenge used digital storytelling to connect climate literacy with everyday action and green-job awareness.

## My role

- Led end-to-end project implementation.
- Designed the training flow and educational content.
- Built pre- and post-assessment methods for monitoring and evaluation.
- Coordinated digital dissemination and participant engagement.
- Integrated green jobs and climate storytelling into the learning experience.

## Outcomes

- **30+** young participants from across Indonesia
- **13** educational vlogs produced
- **20,000+** total vlog views
- **30,000+** impressions
- **77%** workshop participation for both pre- and post-tests
- **14.7%** average knowledge increase in carbon-footprint understanding
- **0.38** N-Gain score, categorized as medium effectiveness

## Approach

The program combined climate education, digital storytelling, and peer-to-peer sharing. The AIDA model was used as a communication framework: Attention → Interest → Desire → Action.

## Key lesson

Digital engagement is most useful when it does more than distribute information—it gives young people a practical way to understand climate issues, create, communicate, and act.
