## Overview

A Master's Applied Climatology research project at IPB University focused on predicting daily surface temperature using a FeedForward Neural Network (FNN).

## Approach

- Preprocessed seasonal patterns, normalized variables, and generated lag features.
- Built an FNN with Keras and tuned hyperparameters with Keras Tuner.
- Examined relationships among temperature, rainfall, humidity, and solar radiation.
- Evaluated performance using Pearson correlation, Taylor diagram, MAE, and RMSE.

## Key result

The model achieved a Pearson correlation of **R = 0.97**. Moving averages also revealed seasonal patterns and climatic shifts in the simulated 1995–2024 weather series.

## Why it matters

The work demonstrates how nonlinear climate systems can be modeled with machine learning to support decision-making in agriculture, disaster preparedness, and local climate adaptation.
